**The Observation**
While the information provided is comprehensive, I have observed that its "scattered" nature—split between PDFs, app notifications, and chat groups—can sometimes make it challenging for parents to track daily priorities efficiently. I imagine this distribution might also be cumbersome for teachers to manage day-to-day.

**The Solution: SchoolPuls**
To bridge this gap, my team and I developed a dedicated application called **SchoolPuls**, initially designed to track my son's daily activities. The goal was simple: to bring the schedule, homework, rhymes, and important events into one intuitive, interactive interface.

I recently shared this tool with the **PP3 Batch (2025-26)** parents, and the response has been overwhelming. Many parents are now using it daily to stay aligned with the curriculum and engage better with their children's learning.
